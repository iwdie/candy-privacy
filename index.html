Candy privacy policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Candy does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app connects only to the OpenCode server that you set up and choose to connect to. The developer runs no servers that receive your data.

About this policy

This policy applies to the Candy Android app (“Candy”, “the app”), developed by Naveen (“I”, “me”). Candy is an unofficial client for OpenCode. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the OpenCode project.

Information the app handles

  • Server connection details. The address of your OpenCode server and any login details you enter for it are stored only on your device, so you don't have to re-enter them.
  • Sessions and messages. Prompts you send, responses, and session or project information travel directly between your device and your own OpenCode server. They are not sent to me or to any other party by Candy.
  • App settings. Preferences such as theme are stored locally on your device.

Candy does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers, and it does not require an account.

Where your data goes

Candy communicates only with the server address you enter. Whatever that server does with your data (including any AI model providers it is configured to use) is governed by that server's setup and the policies of those providers, not by Candy. Please review their policies separately.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Candy contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. Google Play may collect limited information such as install statistics and crash reports under Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

Candy uses network access to connect to your OpenCode server. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party, because Candy does not send your data to me in the first place.

Security

Data stored by the app stays in Candy's private storage on your device. For your safety, connect to your server over HTTPS or a private network such as a VPN wherever possible.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything Candy has stored by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Since I hold no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Candy is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer by opening an issue at github.com/iwdie/candy-privacy/issues.

Candy is an independent, unofficial app and is not affiliated with OpenCode.
