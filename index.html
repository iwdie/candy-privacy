Candy privacy policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Candy does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app connects to the OpenCode server that you set up, plus a voice service only if you choose one. The developer runs no servers that receive your data.

About this policy

This policy applies to the Candy Android app (“Candy”, “the app”), developed by Naveen (“I”, “me”). Candy is an unofficial client for OpenCode. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the OpenCode project.

Information the app handles

  • Server connection details. The address of your OpenCode server, its password or pairing token, and session cookies are stored only on your device so you stay connected.
  • Sessions and messages. Prompts you send, responses, project folders and screenshots your agent saves travel directly between your device and your own OpenCode server. They are not sent to me.
  • Voice. If you use voice dictation or Call Candy, the app records audio from your microphone only while you are speaking to it. How that audio is handled depends on the voice mode you choose (see below).
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code shown by OpenCode. Images are processed on your device and not stored or sent anywhere.
  • App settings. Preferences, drafts and any API key you enter for a voice service are stored locally on your device.

Candy does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or device identifiers, and it does not require an account.

Where your data goes

Candy has no server of its own. Depending on the features you use, data goes only to:

  • Your OpenCode server, at the address you enter. What it does with your data, including any AI model providers it is configured to use, is governed by your own setup and those providers’ policies.
  • Sarvam AI, only if you choose the Sarvam voice mode and enter your own Sarvam API key. Audio is streamed to Sarvam while you dictate, under Sarvam’s own privacy policy.
  • Google’s speech service, only if you choose a Google speech voice mode. Your phone’s speech service may send audio to Google unless offline speech is downloaded, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hugging Face, only to download on-device voice models when you tap Prepare. This is an ordinary file download; no audio or personal content is sent. In the on-device voice modes, audio never leaves your phone.

Typing “demo” as the server address opens a demo mode that uses built-in sample data and sends nothing over the network.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Candy contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. Google Play may collect limited information such as install statistics and crash reports under Google’s privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to your OpenCode server and the services listed above.
  • Camera: to scan the pairing QR code.
  • Microphone: for voice dictation and Call Candy.
  • Audio settings and Bluetooth: to route Call Candy audio to a headset or watch.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party, because Candy does not send your data to me in the first place.

Security

Data stored by the app stays in Candy's private storage on your device. If you use Android's device backup, app settings may be included in your backup. For your safety, connect to your server over HTTPS or a private network such as a VPN wherever possible.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything Candy has stored by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Since I hold no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Candy is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer by opening an issue at github.com/iwdie/candy-privacy/issues.

Candy is an independent, unofficial app and is not affiliated with OpenCode.
